## 0. Summary

Lost Evolution is the third *Digimon Story* title on the NDS Story engine family. Its container
is the same architecture as World DS and Dawn/Dusk (two MD5-headed slots, a write sequence at
`+0x18`, one live copy chosen by sequence), but every geometry and the individual record layout
differ:

- slots are exactly `0x8000` bytes; the signature is `DM3_000` at `+0x10`;
- the save body at `+0x20` is the serialized **player state** (`0x407C` bytes), not a compact
  record table;
- 40 individual records of `0x78` bytes sit at `0x1850`;
- a record carries a u64 identity (low32 = unique id, high32 = `0x030405F0`), an 18-byte name,
  species at `+0x0C`, explicit level at `+0x3C`, one EXP word at `+0x40` and stat words at
  `+0x28..+0x34`/`+0x3D`;
- party membership is an 8-slot u64 identity array at `0x3E8`, farm membership lives in up to four
  group blocks at `0x11F4` (stride `0xD0`, 8 residents each), and a further 8-slot team container
  sits at `0x159C`;
- the next identity is the u64 at `0x4090` (the game's own allocator, ARM9 `FUN_020ad35c`).

All of that was recovered from the ROM's own creator/save code (`FUN_020b0100`,
`FUN_020b0788`, `FUN_020aff40`, `FUN_020af9b8`, `FUN_020aec80`, `FUN_020ade64`, the save
manager at `0x0209c3fc`/`0x0209c670`) and cross-checked against the real save.

## 1. Scope

**In scope**

- Lost Evolution (Japan `BLEJ`, Operation Decoded T-En v1.1 + AP fix) raw 64 KiB `.sav` and the
  first `0x8000`-byte slot exposed as a bare save.
- Full parity ladder: origin list entry, save integrity review, roster with native values,
  reviewed Storage acquisition (party **and** farm residents), species identification, shared
  Home/materialization, destination preview, exact retained move/send, generated/synthesized
  sends, cross-title sends, presence/journal/recovery.
- Both directions: `Lost Evolution → Storage` and `Storage → Lost Evolution`.

**Boundaries (honest)**

- In-game boot acceptance of a written save has not been performed by the user yet; the
  candidates are verified byte-wise (sequence bump, MD5 reseal, readback, byte-exact restore) and
  against the game's own creator/allocator code, but only the game can confirm the final load.
- No ROM, extracted binary or private save is committed; the species asset is generated by a
  tracked script from the user's own ROM.
- Team container (`0x159C`) semantics are not established; records are read as `BANK` and are
  inspection-only for moves.
- EXP-to-next curve and the `+0x3A` aptitude byte semantics are not required by the current
  conversion; synthesis writes explicit stats with EXP 0 and the record's own level.
