# Lore foundation and source ledger

The product is an in-world Tamer companion and research tool. **The whole
Digimon.net About tab is the primary source set**, complemented by official
Seekers, Re:Digitize, and Liberator materials. Source scope remains attached to
each assertion. Research knowledge is separate from individual history, Tamer
notes, device measurements, and app-defined training rules.

## Primary About material

- [About Digimon](https://digimon.net/about/): digital life, Digitama, food and data,
  growth, lifespan, and a network-built world with seas, islands and continents.
  Multiple servers manage it; the identity of human operators and its original
  purpose are unknown. Its public-discovery chronology is an attributed account,
  not a timeline imposed on every secondary narrative.
- [LCD/device history](https://digimon.net/history/): device-specific features,
  including Vital Bracelet activity/heart-rate links, DiM data, Pendulum Jogress,
  Accel Blast Evolution, Xros Loader DigiXros, and evolution guides. Publication
  and production statements do not become a Digimon's biography.
- [Game history](https://digimon-gameportal.bn-ent.net/product/): publication
  records and links to official material, including Re:Digitize and Decode.
- [Digimon Profile](https://digimon.net/profile/): Agumon Hakase's research reports.
  #009 distinguishes species from individuals; #011 describes growth and branching
  evolution; #020 explains ecological attributes; #021 distinguishes Jogress from
  level-raising Jogress Evolution; #027 distinguishes acquired and innate
  X-Antibody; #028 describes ecological families; #032 establishes Witchelny and
  other layers; #035 describes Dark Areas and Cocytus; #036 describes Royal Knights
  and their individual interpretation of orders.
- [Iliad](https://digimon.net/digitalworld-Iliad/): Homeros governs this distinct
  world; the Olympos XII guard separate regions. Its Dark Area is ruled by Plutomon
  and returns functioning DigiCores as Digitama. The Titans are a historical
  community, not a species classification. Research inferences about Greek/Roman
  data and ancient accounts retain their stated uncertainty.
- [Shambhala](https://digimon.net/digitalworld-shambala/): Kunlun is its
  administrator. Genshi Continent, Ashino Island and Yomi have distinct ecologies;
  guardians have regional duties. Susanoomon's rewriting of the world is a
  transmitted creation account, not an observed event in every individual's past.

## Official supplementary observations

- [Seekers terminology](https://digimon.net/digimonseekers/) and
  [chapter 1-4](https://digimon.net/digimonseekers/novel/1-4.php): Digimon are living
  information lifeforms. Digimon Docks store their data; the Digimon Linker is a
  particular AE smartwatch-type Dock. Mindlink is a specific consciousness-transfer
  technology, not a synonym for NFC. Ryusenji distinguishes visualizations generated
  from observations from directly perceiving the Digital World. That narrative's
  limited public awareness remains setting-specific.
- Re:Digitize's official [world description](https://digimon-gameportal.bn-ent.net/product/world_re_digitize/world_world.html)
  presents an incompletely understood data world in another dimension. Its
  [online-game description](https://digimon-gameportal.bn-ent.net/product/world_re_digitize/world_netgame.html)
  identifies GIGO's Digital Monster and its Colosseum. Its
  [DigiStorage guide](https://digimon-gameportal.bn-ent.net/product/world_re_digitize/system03_3.html)
  covers partner records and storage cards. These pages are image-sliced; the
  Japanese text and UI were read from the original images, not inferred from the
  page headings. Specific game mechanics remain specific mechanics.
- Liberator [TIPS 02](https://digimoncard.com/digimon_liberator/en/column/tips02/),
  [04](https://digimoncard.com/digimon_liberator/en/column/tips04/),
  [08](https://digimoncard.com/digimon_liberator/en/column/tips08/),
  [12](https://digimoncard.com/digimon_liberator/en/column/tips12/), and
  [16](https://digimoncard.com/digimon_liberator/en/column/tips16/): D-Storage supports
  communications, a library, partner registration, battle records and tournament
  workflows. The Garden report distinguishes real digital life from manufactured
  NPCs, describes conservation and cardification, and acknowledges unknown origins.
  Lacuna, the Garden and T.A.L.E. retain that setting's boundaries; the app's farm
  does not acquire their identity or capabilities merely through inspiration.

## Product interpretation

- Published ecology establishes the common vocabulary; realm-specific reports
  establish local institutions and limits. Yggdrasill is not every world's host,
  and one world's underworld/rebirth account does not replace another's.
- Species profiles do not establish an individual's birthplace, office, memories,
  relationships or canonical character identity. Newly scanned individuals begin
  with their own records. Recorded conversation is an interaction record, not
  proof of every unobserved historical claim made in dialogue.
- Catalog name/sprite matching is not official-source verification. Existing
  manual profiles and Tamer notes retain their authorship. Citation-scraping and
  production artifacts are separated from ordinary biological descriptions.
- Training reconstructions allow documented Tamer teams to coexist as challenges.
  Combat-tier scaling changes battle parameters, not species identity or biology.
  Jogress, DigiXros and mode changes keep their distinct terminology where known.
- UI and partner speech use an in-world voice. Developer identifiers, experiments,
  raw instructions, implementation provenance and exception details require Debug.
  Useful requirements, compatibility facts, recovery and exact readings remain
  available in normal details.

Sources were inspected during the October 2026 overhaul. The source ledger records
the pages and claim boundaries actually used; it does not claim exhaustive coverage
of every novel chapter, comic episode, or species record.
