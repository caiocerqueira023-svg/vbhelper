# Battle screen — finish review: Decode 3×3 command band

- Surface: `Battle screen` (training/Arena, Radar, AR stage), Operate, native Android/Compose.
- Contract: `.impeccable/surfaces/ui-offline-offlinetrainingbattlescreen-kt-748dab41.md` (THESIS / OWN-WORLD / STORY / FIRST VIEWPORT / FORM / FINISH), `DESIGN.md` → Battle screen (lines 718–767), `PRODUCT.md`.
- Scope of evidence: **source review plus arithmetic only**. No screenshot, emulator, device, TalkBack or font-scale pass exists for this increment. The build/test numbers below are the delivery's own report and were not re-run in this review.

## Verdict

**Revise before native acceptance. The contract is largely met in code; the finish is not proven and four P1 gaps are code-evident.**

What the code actually delivers: exactly nine fixed positions in the requested order (`BattleCommandDeckModel.kt:11–21`, rendered at `BattleChrome.kt:537–547`); the partner's HP/MP/ESP moved into the band header (`BattleChrome.kt:456–482`) and the floating partner strip is gone from `BattleCombatantHud`; CP and the cheer/Support command are removed from core, UI, strings and feed, while the homonyms survive (`BattleStrategy.SUPPORT`, `TamerStyle.SUPPORT`, `TechniqueKind.SUPPORT/HEAL`); Move is a real one-tap order with arm/cancel/invalid/ordered states and simulator clamping; the Blast panel has no Stop button and any touch on its own surface resolves. Those are contract hits, not intentions.

What keeps this out of finish: the Blast "any touch" surface is **not the topmost layer** and the chrome above it remains live; the 3-row band is not adapted to short/landscape windows, where it also makes the enemy plates unplaceable; the AR branch puts the same 3×3 inside a scrolling column that can hide its third row; and Move has no path at all for a TalkBack user. Several P2s are smaller contract drift (ESP conditional, heal/support special targeting, wide-window header, disabled-state reasons, spacing, copy).

## Material findings

### P0

None. Nothing here is a build or crash blocker; no data or migration risk was found.

### P1

1. **The Blast timing surface is not the input surface the instruction promises.**
   `BlastTimingOverlay` is a full-screen clickable `Box` (`OfflineTrainingBattleScreen.kt:2268–2277`) that deliberately sits *under* the later chrome. In training the top cluster is declared at `:566` and the band at `:650`; in the full-bleed Radar branch at `:1522` and `:1579`. Those siblings draw above the 42% scrim and receive input first. The top-cluster pause/exit/recenter keys stay enabled during the window (`BattleChrome.kt:218–259`; `recenterEnabled` only excludes finishers), so tapping Pause or Exit during the timing answers the chrome, not the timing; tapping Recenter even changes the camera. The band keys are disabled but the band is painted on top and reads as live. The overlay has no `paneTitle` or modal semantics, so TalkBack also does not treat it as an interrupting surface. "Any touch answers it — no Stop button" is true only for the middle slice of the screen.
   In the AR branch the overlay is composed inside the square viewport (`WorldRadarBattleHud`, `:1751–1767`), while the command column lives outside it — the square, not the screen, is the input.
   Fix direction: draw the timing panel above every other sibling (or make it a dialog/semantics pane), disable/remove the top-cluster keys while it is open, and make the AR path use the same full-window panel.

2. **On short/landscape windows the band eats the arena and the enemy plates disappear.**
   The band is a fixed 3-row grid plus header/vitals/feedback; measured from the constants and type styles it is ≈241dp at font scale 1.0 (≈265–289dp with the navigation-bar inset) and only the paddings and inter-key gaps shrink under `compactHeight` (`BattleChrome.kt:608–609`, `:622–646`, `:687–807`). It never reflows rows or changes key height per window. The top cluster reserves ≈81–88dp (`BattleChrome.kt:109`; `OfflineTrainingBattleScreen.kt:420–422`). On a 640×360dp landscape phone (there is no orientation lock: `AndroidManifest.xml:59–63`; `enableEdgeToEdge` at `MainActivity.kt:92`; DESIGN says landscape shares one structure) that leaves roughly 30–40dp of unobstructed arena. Worse, `BattleCombatantHud` clamps placements to `topLimit`/`bottomLimit` (`BattleChrome.kt:333–334`) and silently skips a plate whose rectangle does not fit (`:368–374`); with `bottomLimit ≈ 360 − 241 − 29 = 90dp` and `topLimit ≈ 102dp`, **no enemy name/HP plate can be placed at all**. This is arithmetic from the code, not a rendered observation — but it does not depend on camera or content. The same band is the only command surface in landscape.
   Fix direction: a short/landscape variant (2 rows or a side rail, smaller header, no fixed 48dp min per row when the window is short) and a documented minimum window height that the layout checks instead of silently dropping the HUD.

3. **The AR command deck can be below the fold, and it scrolls as a whole.**
   `WorldRadarBattleContent`'s AR branch composes the square viewport (`OfflineTrainingBattleScreen.kt:1177–1190`), a 12dp spacer and the old 48dp Pause/Tactics/Exit row, then the fixed band inside `Column(Modifier.weight(1f, fill = false).verticalScroll(...))` (`:1333–1337`, `:1374–1412`). On a 360×640dp window the square is 328dp, the remaining command area is about 232dp, while the row + band need about 307dp; roughly 75dp of the deck starts below the fold. In compact landscape the square is capped at 140dp and the deficit grows to >100dp. The third row (Focus/Defend/Move) is then only reachable by scrolling, which moves the target under the thumb and breaks the "fixed 3×3 muscle memory" promise. The surface brief lists the AR stage as out of scope, but DESIGN says AR inherits the deck, and this delivery touched the AR band; the P1 applies to that branch as shipped.
   Fix direction: give AR the same bottom-anchored overlay band as the other two paths, or a landscape/AR-specific compact deck that is guaranteed visible without scroll.

4. **Move is pointer-only for TalkBack and has no accessible destination path.**
   `ArenaMoveLayer` is an empty full-screen `Box` with only `pointerInput`/`detectTapGestures` and no semantics (`OfflineTrainingBattleScreen.kt:2446–2466`). Arming Move announces "Touch the arena to move" (`BattleChrome.kt:794–798`), but a screen-reader user has no way to choose a point. `accessibleBattle` provides fighter-selection custom actions only (`BattleAccessibility.kt:24–34`); there is no destination action, no preset, and the 14dp `MoveTargetMarker` is not announced. The contract explicitly requires TalkBack semantics for the command vocabulary; Move currently fails that for the trainer who relies on it.
   Fix direction: expose arena destinations as custom actions (e.g., ally-relative compass/quadrant points or a simple grid) and announce the resulting order; otherwise record an explicit accessibility exception against the surface contract.

### P2

1. **A healing/support special is enabled but always rejected (latent target bug).** `onSpecial` passes `selectedOpponent?.combatantId` in all three branches (`OfflineTrainingBattleScreen.kt:665`, `:1394`, `:1599`), while `BattleCommandBand` enables a special with `healPower > 0` even with no enemy (`BattleChrome.kt:594–598`) and the simulator classifies `SPECIAL && healPower > 0` as support and requires a same-side target (`BattleSimulator.kt:270`, `:1854–1855`). The player's generic special has `healPower = 0` today, so this is latent for the band; `TamerBattleLoadouts.kt:113–119` does create such a special, so the path exists and the UI implies it is usable. `techniqueTargetFor` (`:2434–2444`) also omits `TechniqueKind.SUPPORT` even though the simulator and the band do not. Use one target rule for ordinary techniques and specials, and include SUPPORT in it.
2. **ESP is conditional, so the header is not stable and the Special key can be permanently dead.** `PartnerVitalsBand` hides the ESP instrument when `specialTechniqueId == null` (`BattleChrome.kt:472–480`), while the Special key remains in the grid (`:737–755`) showing "Special · 0%"/charging forever. `CombatantSnapshot.specialTechniqueId` is nullable (`BattleModel.kt:220`). This contradicts "the header holds HP/MP/ESP" and makes the header reflow between partners; if every shipping partner has a special, say so and assert it, or show ESP at 0 rather than removing the instrument.
3. **The transient Move marker marks the raw tap, not the point the simulator accepted.** `moveMarker = screenOffset` (`OfflineTrainingBattleScreen.kt:550`, `:1312`, `:1507`) while the core clamps to `arenaRadius − collisionRadius` (`BattleSimulator.kt:210`, `:1692–1693`). Near the edge, the cyan marker appears outside the playable floor while the partner stops inside it. Project the clamped destination (or drop the marker and rely on the band line, as the surface brief's "feedback is the band line and the partner's own movement" says).
4. **Wide windows split the band's axis.** The deck is capped at 520dp and centred (`BattleChrome.kt:687–690`), but the header, vitals and feedback rows stretch the full window (`:622–646`). On a tablet, HP/MP/ESP hairline tracks become hundreds of dp wide while the nine keys stay in a narrow centred block; the band reads as two different instruments. Cap the whole content column to the same 520dp and let only the scrim stay full-bleed.
5. **Disabled keys do not say why, and the state channel is visual-only.** Availability depends on cooldown and reserved energy (`BattleChrome.kt:579–587`, `:594–598`), but a blocked technique only dims its border/text (`:835–849`); there is no cooldown or EN-missing reason, and the TalkBack description is the bare action label (`:825–833`). DESIGN/contract promise "name + EN cost + state". Add a state line/`stateDescription` (e.g., cooldown remaining) or, at minimum, expose the disabled reason to accessibility.
6. **Key spacing is below the native floor: 4–6dp between 48dp keys** (`BattleChrome.kt:633`, `:690`, `:695`). The native reference asks for at least 8dp between touch targets; at 360dp width the grid has room for 8dp (3×≈106dp keys + 2×8dp + 24dp padding). A 3×3 thumb grid is exactly where spacing mistakes cause misfires.
7. **The Blast "red line" is an 18% red block and the timing has no non-visual cue.** `BLAST_RED_ZONE_START = 0.82f` (`OfflineTrainingBattleScreen.kt:2207`) fills the last 18% as a rectangle (`:2365–2369`), but the instruction in all three locales says "red line" (`values/strings.xml:974`, pt/ja equivalents) and the contract/surface brief says "red target line". The overlay/bar carries no `contentDescription`/`stateDescription` and no sound or haptic start cue, so the timing challenge is unusable for TalkBack even though the full-screen button exists. Decide line vs zone and make the copy match; add an accessible timing cue or document the exception.
8. **Band copy repeats itself and the training top cluster is not the matchup.** The header says "COMMANDS" (`BattleChrome.kt:641`, `values/strings.xml:830`) and the feedback fallback says "Battle commands" (`:657`; string at `:944`) directly below it; partner name is what matters there. Training keeps "Offline battle training" as the title (`OfflineTrainingBattleScreen.kt:567`) although DESIGN's top cluster requires the matchup. Menu keys append a Unicode "›" as an icon (`:728`, `:758`), which the craft floor rejects in favour of a drawn icon.
9. **The AR branch still ships the retired standing toggle row.** Pause / "Tactics ON-OFF" / Exit (`OfflineTrainingBattleScreen.kt:1344–1372`, string `ui_battle_tactics_state`) contradicts DESIGN's "Pause lives in the top cluster; the standing Tactics toggle is now the tactical-pause switch inside the pause panel". AR is the declared legacy exception, but the result is three different topologies for the same battle surface; either accept and document it explicitly or unify it.
10. **The deck model carries a dead `moveArmed` field and the test that names it proves nothing.** `BattleCommandAvailability.moveArmed` is never read by `battleCommandDeck()` (`BattleCommandDeckModel.kt:29–31`, `:38–50`); `moveArmedHighlightsWithoutRemovingOrReorderingAnySlot` only asserts the order/enabled lists are unchanged (`BattleCommandDeckModelTest.kt:114–123`). The highlight is actually owned by the screen, so the "highlight" contract is untested. Delete the field or return the highlighted slot and assert it.

## What a device render must still prove

No render exists; these are the checks the code cannot close. Run them on a real device (the reported one disconnected) with `adb`, not on a browser:

1. **Portrait, 360×640dp and 411×891dp, font scale 1.0 and 1.3**: band height, visible arena area, all nine keys ≥48dp and inside the window, technique names and partner name not ellipsized or clipped, HP/MP/ESP values legible as tabular numerals, no key row moving between battle states.
2. **Landscape 640×360dp and split-screen ≈600×360dp**: where the band's top edge lands, whether enemy name/HP plates appear at all (the code predicts none), whether any key is off-screen, and whether the result panel fits. This is the configuration P1-2 predicts will fail.
3. **AR stage, portrait and compact landscape**: the full 3×3 visible without scrolling; the square's size; the Blast panel actually covering the whole screen; the old Pause/Tactics row's role.
4. **Blast input coverage**: tap the top bar (pause, exit, recenter), the band, the panel, and the arena during an open window and record which one answers. Also prove the bar's red target is legible against every palette at the exact moment of the sweep, and that the needle is visible for reduced-motion users.
5. **Every saved palette** (Tamers Lab, VB Lab, VB Arena, Digimon.net): contrast of the header text and hairline vitals over the live scene; enabled vs disabled vs armed key states; the red target line. The header now sits on the band's near-transparent top scrim instead of a surface, so this is the specific pixel question the source cannot answer.
6. **TalkBack**: 9 keys in order with names/costs/disabled state, the merged HP/MP/ESP sentence, the armed Move announcement, the Blast instruction and whether the panel is announced as a modal, the pause panel, and a destination path for Move (P1-4). Compilation is not acceptance.
7. **Move accuracy**: tap known floor points in portrait and in AR (near centre, near each arena edge, above the horizon, on the stands); verify the partner reaches the clamped point under the finger and that the marker matches it. The pure projection test cannot prove the Compose layer's coordinate origin, the camera's live matrix, or the AR 2dp inset.
8. **Insets**: gesture and 3-button navigation, status bar, landscape cutout, and the band's bottom inset; the band, top cluster and result panel must stay inside the safe area in all three layouts.
